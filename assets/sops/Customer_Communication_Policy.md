# BizPilot AI — Customer Communication Guidelines & Policies

**Document ID:** POL-OPS-002  
**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** Small Business Operations & Multi-channel Support

---

## 1. Principles of Customer Engagement
1. **Accurate Data Only:** Never guess or hallucinate delivery dates, tracking numbers, or refund amounts. Always verify against live records in the database.
2. **Empathy and Clarity:** Acknowledge delays transparently. Give customers clear next steps and direct contact channels.
3. **Accountability:** Every unfulfilled commitment or delayed delivery MUST have an associated actionable Task assigned with an explicit priority and due date.

## 2. Order Status Definitions
- `pending`: Order received and paid, awaiting fulfillment assignment.
- `processing`: Warehouse picking, packing, or packaging in progress.
- `shipped`: Dispatched via logistics courier with tracking active.
- `delivered`: Courier handover confirmed at destination address.
- `delayed`: Shipment stalled due to inventory replenishment, transit delay, or weather disruption.
- `cancelled`: Order refunded or cancelled by customer / administrator.

## 3. Escalation Thresholds
- Any order delayed > 48 hours without courier status scan requires immediate management notification and priority task creation.
